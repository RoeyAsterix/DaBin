DaBin / Launch playbook

One useful story at a time.

A practical 30-day plan, starting when DaBin is publicly downloadable.

Day 1 is your verified publication date. This is a relative plan, not a scheduled automation. Move publication tasks to match actual readiness and platform rules. Keep a daily reply check during launch week.

Before Day 1

At least 3 weeks ahead

Prepare the actual Apple featuring launch nomination where practical. Nomination is editorial consideration, not a promise.

One week ahead

Complete URLs, support, privacy, final-build comparison, fictional examples and current platform-rule checks.

The day before

Watch the final clips with sound and muted. Check crops, subtitles, compatibility and every destination link.

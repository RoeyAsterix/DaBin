DaBin landing page — local draft, not published

Open index.html after extracting the whole marketing ZIP. All media paths are
relative, and the page uses system fonts. No network font, tracker, analytics,
form submission, or automatic upload is installed.

After the app is available:
1. Set appStoreURL to the real https://apps.apple.com/... link in config.js.
2. Set contactEmail to the public support email you wish to use.
3. Verify privacyURL is published, maintained and matches the shipping app.
4. Recheck the product statements against the released build.
5. Deploy only when you choose a hosting destination. Copy only index.html,
   config.js, captions.vtt and the media/ directory into a separate public
   deployment folder. These paths are self-contained. Do not publish the whole
   marketing kit, editable source, provider IDs, outreach or QA records.

With a valid App Store URL, download buttons activate and the draft banner is
removed. The page does not invent an App Store ID or route to an unrelated app.

The provided English captions.vtt works best when served over HTTP/HTTPS;
some browsers restrict external caption files when opened via file://.

The video is the guided DaBin UI tour with conversational Andre Rene narration, synchronized robot speech/gaze and pointer guidance over actual native states. The main media filenames use DABIN__UI_TOUR__. The matching94.4-second MP4, poster and English captions are integrated in this draft.

This page is an editable HTML marketing asset. It was not hosted or submitted.

Rebuild Feed DaBin locally

Requirements: Blender 5.x, Python 3.11+ with Pillow and numpy, public FFmpeg.
The textures use system Arial fonts from macOS; change FONT/BOLD if needed.

From this folder:
  python3 prepare_video.py
  blender -b --python build_feed_dabin.py -- --layout landscape --render
  blender -b --python build_feed_dabin.py -- --layout portrait --render
  python3 encode_video.py

Use DABIN_FFMPEG to specify FFmpeg when it is not on PATH.
Intermediate frame directories are created under the kit's .work directory.
The ZIP excludes those frames. Scripts render all 660 frames at native size.

Packed .blend files contain exact robot/native UI textures and editable objects,
keyframes, orthographic camera, emission materials and timing. The MP4 audio is
mixed after rendering; it is not stored as a Blender sound strip. Select take02
and paced WAV are supplied. The .blend files can render without running the
Python asset generator; the generator is provided to revise text and timing.

Look in storyboard.json for the shot sequence and in 07_QA for encoded-frame
contact sheets and full decode verification. This is a code-authored editorial
animation; no model-generated app interface or robot redesign is used.

DABIN - FRIENDLY PRODUCT COPY

A little robot for the useful bits of your day.

DaBin keeps your ideas, links, images and files together on your Mac. Drop something onto the robot, keep it with a project, and find it again when you need it.

ROBOT-LED QUICK GUIDE

Hi, I'm DaBin.
I keep the useful bits of your day - an idea, a link, an image or a file - so you can find them again and get back to work.

Catch me in a corner.
Move your pointer to a screen corner. Drop something onto my body, or hover over me and press Command-V. Double-click me to open DaBin.
Prefer the camera island? Choose it in Settings. On other displays, I'll use the top-right corner.

Choose a project
Pick a home for related work. Give it a color, and I'll frame its cards to match.

Your three starting points
Inbox is for organizing. Today is for doing. Projects keeps related work together.

Find it again
Use Search when you remember a word but not where you saved it.

See what you saved
Explorer puts previews first. Open a card for its details; click a file preview there to open my saved copy.

Save it. Find it. Pick it up again.
You don't need to learn everything at once. Start with these three small steps.

Save now. Sort later.
Drop or paste something into Inbox. Open a card to add a note or choose its project.
Day and Week help you look back through your captures by date.

Keep a project together.
Choose a project, then browse its saved things in Explorer. Its color helps you spot what belongs together.
Use Notes for your thoughts, Clipboard for copied things, and Shelf for files you want close by.

Give one task your attention.
Set a task's focus time and press Play. When time's up, I hold an alarm clock and a sign with the task's first three words.
Click me to send me home. Your task stays open until you mark it done.

Want a little help saving?
Auto Capture is optional and off by default. In Settings, choose Clipboard, Screenshots, or both. Screenshots need a folder you choose. Only new changes are saved.
Check Destination in the window: new automatic captures go to that project, or Unfiled. Pause whenever you like.

Make yourself comfortable.
Drag an empty spot at the top to move the window. Resize from an edge or corner, or use Expand. Hover for little hints; turn off Show tooltips in Settings if you prefer.

Your things stay on your Mac.
No account or cloud sync. Link previews only contact websites if you turn them on.

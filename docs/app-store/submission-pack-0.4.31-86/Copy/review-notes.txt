DaBin is an Apple Silicon macOS menu-bar/desktop app. It requires macOS 14 or later and has no login, paid unlock, ads or server account.

Start: Open DaBin; use its menu-bar item or double-click the desktop robot to open the board. The upper + opens a new task directly. Quit through the menu bar or Settings. Closing the board leaves the menu-bar app running; Quit shuts its services down.

Manual capture: Paste text or a link with Cmd-V, or drop a fictional image/file into the app. Create a note/task, assign a project and search for its content. Search accepts typing, context-menu paste and Cmd-V; results are grouped by date. Export and archive backup use native destination pickers. Local OCR/document search is limited to saved supported content.

Auto Capture: OFF on first launch. Enable it through its explicit consent flow, choose clipboard and/or screenshots, and choose a destination. Screenshot monitoring requires selecting a folder in the macOS picker; it monitors only new regular images, not existing files or the live display. The menu-bar icon becomes a red recording symbol while monitoring, even with the board closed or Quiet mode on. Ready, Paused and Off are separate states. Pause or turn off to stop monitoring. Automatically captured links never fetch remote previews. Source-app exclusions and sensitive-pasteboard checks are best-effort.

Privacy: Settings → Privacy policy & data controls contains the full bundled policy, local deletion instructions and online policy/support links. Captures are stored on device. Website previews are optional, off by default, and only apply to manually saved links. There is no analytics, advertising or cloud-sync SDK. The app does not request Accessibility, Screen Recording, camera or microphone access. Reminder permission is requested only when a reminder is saved; notifications use generic text. A completed focus timer can show the first three words of the task title locally.

Store build: App Sandbox is enabled. User-selected files and scoped bookmarks support import/export and screenshot-folder access. The direct-download updater and installer are excluded from this binary; updates are managed by the Mac App Store. No automatic launch-at-login or privileged helper is installed.

Suggested review: verify manual save/search/task/project/export; opt into clipboard capture using fictional text; opt into a new screenshot in a chosen temporary folder; pause and confirm monitoring stops; disable capture and quit. No demo credentials are needed.

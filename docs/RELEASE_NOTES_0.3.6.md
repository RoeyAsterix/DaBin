# DaBin 0.3.6

DaBin 0.3.6 gives every individual caption card a clear, lightweight boundary while keeping the board compact.

## Rounded caption cards

Individual cards in Daily, Search and Reminders now use a thin continuous frame with softly rounded corners. The frame remains visible in light and dark appearances without overpowering the content.

Unfinished carried tasks keep their stronger theme-colour outline. Grouped captures, hourly summaries and Weekly cards retain their rounded card treatment. An action expanded inside an hourly group uses the hour's existing action frame, avoiding a distracting double border.

The compact Daily panel accounts for the added spacing between cards, including during filter transitions and offscreen-window recovery.

## Update

From DaBin 0.3.5, open **Settings → Software updates**, choose **Check for updates**, then **Download & install**. DaBin verifies the release size and SHA-256 checksum before its installer asks to replace the app. Your capture archive stays in place.

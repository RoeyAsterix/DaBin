DABIN - FRIENDLY PRODUCT COPY

Version 0.4.31 (86)

A little robot for the useful bits of your day.

DaBin keeps your ideas, links, images and files together on your Mac. Drop something onto the robot, keep it with a project, and find it again when you need it.

ROBOT-LED QUICK GUIDE

Hi, I'm DaBin.
I keep the useful bits of your day - an idea, a link, an image or a file - so you can find them again and get back to work.

Catch me in a corner.
Move your pointer to a screen corner. Drop something onto my body, or hover over me and press Command-V. Double-click me to open DaBin.
Prefer the camera island? Choose it in Settings. On other displays, I'll use the top-right corner.

Choose a project
Keep notes, tasks, links and files together. Use All, Files or Tasks to narrow the workspace.

Three places to begin
Inbox catches new things. Today helps you focus. Projects keeps related work together.

Search all saved work
Click Search to look across your saved DaBin content, even when you start inside a project.

Take work with you
Export project saves a local ZIP. Select cards to export just those items. The nearby menu opens the project folder in Finder.

Save it. Find it. Pick it up again.
Three small habits, plus a little help when you want it.

Find it by a few words.
In Search, type or paste a word. Matches line up in date columns, newest first. Use filters when you want to narrow things down.
Search covers your saved content. Files can contribute locally extracted text when available.

Save now. Sort later.
Drop or paste something into Inbox. Open a card to add a comment or choose its project.
Drag a card preview, title or caption into an app that accepts that content. Your saved original stays here.

Give one task your attention.
Click + to open New task. Priority tags go from green (Low), through amber (Medium), to red (High).
Set a focus time and press Play. When time is up, the robot reminds you; mark the task done when you finish.

A little help saving? Your choice.
Auto Capture is off by default. In Settings, choose Clipboard, Screenshots, or both; screenshots need a folder you select. It saves new changes, not a recording of your screen.
When running, the header's red dot grows; it dances unless Reduce Motion is on. Check Destination; pause anytime.

Make yourself comfortable.
Drag an empty spot at the top to move between monitors. Resize from an edge or use Expand. Saved content stays on this Mac. Website previews are optional.

Your things stay on your Mac.
No account or cloud sync. Link previews only contact websites if you turn them on.

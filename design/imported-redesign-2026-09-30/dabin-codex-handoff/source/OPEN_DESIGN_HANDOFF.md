# DaBin redesign handoff for Open Design

The current handoff covers **DaBin 0.4.2 (53)** and requires preserving every implemented feature.

Start with [the redesign brief](design/open-design-handoff-2026-09-30/BRIEF.md), then use the [feature preservation matrix](design/open-design-handoff-2026-09-30/FEATURES.csv), [screen checklist](design/open-design-handoff-2026-09-30/SCREENS_AND_STATES.md), and [acceptance scenarios](design/open-design-handoff-2026-09-30/ACCEPTANCE.md).

The [copyable Open Design prompt](design/open-design-handoff-2026-09-30/OPEN_DESIGN_PROMPT.txt) is included in the portable handoff ZIP.

The earlier brief is preserved under `docs/design-history/OPEN_DESIGN_HANDOFF-pre-0.4.2.md` for history only. Its restrictions against tasks, a composer, or grouped file cards are obsolete and must not be used for the redesign.
